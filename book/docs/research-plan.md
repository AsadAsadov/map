# Research Plan

## Research workflow

1. Extract every factual claim from draft chapters into `book/research/fact-check.md`.
2. Classify each claim as scientific, philosophical, religious, historical or editorial.
3. Verify scientific claims against primary or institutional sources where possible.
4. Verify Islamic references directly against Qurani-Kərim and reputable tafsir/hadith resources.
5. Mark unresolved claims as `SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED`.
6. Remove or soften claims that cannot be verified.

## Scientific source priority

1. NASA, ESA, CERN, NSF and other official scientific institutions.
2. National Academies and official university resources.
3. Peer-reviewed papers and review articles.
4. Nature and Science.
5. Scientific American only for secondary explanation, not final authority.

## Islamic source priority

1. Qurani-Kərim with exact surah and ayah number.
2. Reliable hadith collections when necessary.
3. Classical tafsir with contextual reading.
4. Academic Islamic studies for historical/theological diversity.

## Quality gates

A chapter cannot move to final layout until:

- all factual claims are logged;
- unresolved claims are marked or removed;
- Quran/hadith references are exact;
- diagrams have data sources;
- opposing views are represented fairly;
- AI-like repetition and formulaic endings are edited out.
