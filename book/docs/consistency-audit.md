# Consistency Audit — Phase 3

## ISSUE-001
Chapters: 7, 23, 25
Problem: One thread may say consciousness is strongly linked to the brain, while afterlife chapters discuss survival language.
Type: Science/religion boundary
Severity: HIGH
Recommended Resolution: State scientific evidence and religious belief in separate labeled paragraphs; never say neuroscience proves or disproves axirət.

## ISSUE-002
Chapters: 5, 6, 16, 18
Problem: “Elm bilmir” can be misused as a gap for theological proof.
Type: Category mistake
Severity: HIGH
Recommended Resolution: Unknown scientific mechanisms should remain unknowns; theological meaning must be argued independently.

## ISSUE-003
Chapters: 10, 20
Problem: Determinism, fatalism and Islamic qadar can collapse into one simplified claim.
Type: Conceptual conflation
Severity: MEDIUM
Recommended Resolution: Define determinism, fatalism, foreknowledge and qadar separately.

## ISSUE-004
Chapters: 3, 17, 27
Problem: Cosmic scale may be used alternately as insignificance and as design evidence.
Type: Interpretive inconsistency
Severity: MEDIUM
Recommended Resolution: Treat scale as scientific fact; existential and theological implications as debated interpretations.

## ISSUE-005
Chapters: 19, 20, 26
Problem: Lifespan inequality may be explained too quickly by qadar/axirət.
Type: Pastoral/theological risk
Severity: HIGH
Recommended Resolution: Keep grief and injustice visible; religious response must not erase empirical public-health causes.
