# Islam Research Notes

## Working rule

Use Qurani-Kərim as the primary source. Do not use ayat as fake scientific proof. Separate tafsir, theology and personal reflection.

## Source categories

- Qurani-Kərim: exact surah and ayah number required.
- Hadith: collection, book/chapter and grading/authority notes required.
- Classical tafsir: author, work, passage and interpretive context required.
- Kalam/theology: school and historical context required.
- Modern academic Islamic studies: publication details required.

## Themes to verify

### Human knowing and not knowing
- Potentially relevant: Quran 16:78 — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
- Use only after checking Arabic/Azerbaijani translation context.

### Creation and human origin
- Adam, clay/earth language, divine creation.
- Note diversity of contemporary Muslim approaches to evolution.

### Ruh and nafs
- Avoid over-defining ruh beyond source warrant.
- Potentially relevant ayat require exact verification.

### Qeyb and iman
- Faith in the unseen.
- Avoid presenting faith as anti-reason.

### Qədər and choice
- Present fatalism as one reduction, not the whole tradition.
- Research Ashari, Maturidi and Mu'tazili positions with reliable sources.

### Suffering and justice
- Sabr, imtahan, divine justice, afterlife.
- Do not use suffering to silence grief.

### Death and afterlife
- Barzakh, resurrection, judgment, paradise and hell.
- Exact Quran references and tafsir context required.

## Red lines

- No invented ayah references.
- No invented hadith.
- No “Quran scientifically predicted X” claims without rigorous evidence and scholarly context.
- No anthropomorphic imagery of Allah.
