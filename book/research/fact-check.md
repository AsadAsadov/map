# Fact-Check Register

Use this file for every serious factual claim before it enters final manuscript text.

## Format

```text
CLAIM:
SOURCE:
SOURCE TYPE:
CONFIDENCE:
NOTES:
```

## Initial high-priority claims to verify

CLAIM:
The universe is approximately 13.8 billion years old.
SOURCE:
NASA / ESA / Planck cosmology literature — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED before manuscript use.
SOURCE TYPE:
Scientific consensus.
CONFIDENCE:
Very High after verification.
NOTES:
Use current official NASA/ESA wording and avoid false precision.

CLAIM:
The universe is expanding.
SOURCE:
NASA / ESA cosmology resources and peer-reviewed cosmology literature — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
SOURCE TYPE:
Scientific consensus.
CONFIDENCE:
Very High after verification.
NOTES:
Explain expansion of space carefully; avoid implying galaxies expand internally.

CLAIM:
Many elements in the human body were produced by earlier generations of stars and stellar explosions or related astrophysical processes.
SOURCE:
NASA astrophysics resources and peer-reviewed nucleosynthesis literature — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
SOURCE TYPE:
Scientific consensus with nuance.
CONFIDENCE:
High after verification.
NOTES:
Avoid oversimplified “everything came from supernovae”; hydrogen has different origin.

CLAIM:
The origin of life on Earth is not yet fully explained by science.
SOURCE:
Peer-reviewed origin-of-life review literature — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
SOURCE TYPE:
Active research area.
CONFIDENCE:
High.
NOTES:
Separate abiogenesis hypotheses from established evolution.

CLAIM:
Evolution by natural selection is a central mechanism explaining biological diversity.
SOURCE:
National Academies / peer-reviewed evolutionary biology — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
SOURCE TYPE:
Scientific consensus.
CONFIDENCE:
Very High after verification.
NOTES:
When discussing human evolution, avoid ladder imagery.

CLAIM:
Consciousness is strongly linked to brain activity, but no complete scientific theory explains subjective experience.
SOURCE:
Peer-reviewed neuroscience and consciousness literature — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
SOURCE TYPE:
Scientific consensus plus open philosophical/scientific problem.
CONFIDENCE:
Medium-High.
NOTES:
Distinguish neural correlates from full explanation.

CLAIM:
Brain death is a medical/legal criterion for death in many modern systems.
SOURCE:
Official medical guidelines and peer-reviewed clinical literature — SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
SOURCE TYPE:
Medical consensus with jurisdictional nuance.
CONFIDENCE:
High after verification.
NOTES:
Do not overgeneralize across all countries or legal systems.
